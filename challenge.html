What could you challenge us with?

Problems where crystal design matters.

Property control

Can a molecular material be made more soluble, more stable, easier to process, or better suited to its application?

Crystallisation behaviour

Are nucleation, growth, polymorphism, solvate formation or reproducibility creating a development bottleneck?

Solid-form design

Could a solid solution offer a new route to tuning a material without changing its core molecular identity?

How it works

Bring us the challenge. We bring the community.

1

Tell us the problem

Share the material or system, the property you want to control and what success would look like.

2

We identify the expertise

MoSS spans crystallisation, crystal engineering, characterisation, modelling, robotics, data and AI across a broad collaborative network.

3

We explore a route forward

Depending on the challenge, this could lead to a scientific discussion, feasibility work, a student project or a longer-term collaboration.

A challenge worth solving?

We would like to hear it.

Early conversations can be exploratory. If your challenge involves confidential information, tell us before sharing sensitive details and we can discuss an appropriate route.
