Outreach & engagement

Crystal science for everyone.

MoSS wants to make crystallisation, molecular materials and the ideas behind solid solutions understandable, useful and exciting beyond the research community.

Outreach in action

Sharing crystal science with different audiences.

From museum visitors and festival audiences to primary-school pupils, each activity connects the hidden structures of crystals with the materials people meet every day.

Planned for 202712 primary schools

Crystal Detectives

MoSS and CrystoGen are developing hands-on sessions for around 360 Year 4 pupils in Manchester, Leeds and Durham. Pupils will examine crystal shapes, build lattice models and grow salt crystals at home.

Six schools in Manchester, three in Leeds and three in Durham.

18 May 2026Pint of Science, Durham

How Crystals Secretly Shape Our World

Aurora Cruz-Cabeza showed how crystals influence everything from ice and chocolate to medicines and microchips, and explained why crystal structure is central to the materials around us.
